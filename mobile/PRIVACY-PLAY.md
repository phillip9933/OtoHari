# OtoHari Android privacy policy

**Scope:** OtoHari for Android distributed through Google Play. OtoHari is an independent player for a DroppedNeedle server that you or someone you trust operates. It does not provide a hosted music account.

## Connection and playback

OtoHari sends credentials or session authorizations to the server and identity provider you choose so you can sign in. It uses your session to request the library, music, artwork, account data and playlists. Search terms, playlist edits and playback activity can be sent to your server so it can provide those features. Your server administrator and identity provider determine server-side access, retention and logging. Use only a server you trust.

## Your device and optional integrations

Your device stores login material using platform secure storage. The app can store library metadata, preferences and media you save for offline playback on the device. When you use Android Auto, track information, artwork and playback controls are available to the car interface. When you choose Google Cast, the Cast SDK and selected device handle discovery and playback; they may receive media metadata or connection information needed to play the selection. If you enable a server-side integration, such as scrobbling, that server and provider handle their own data retention and privacy.

You may export or share lists, links or a troubleshooting report. Review reports before sending them and avoid including private server details in a public issue. Data you knowingly share with another app or person is handled by that recipient.

## Removal and contact

Sign out to remove the active session from the app. Use Device storage to remove offline music. Uninstalling removes app-private data, but not files you exported or data kept on your server. For server account deletion or server-side history, contact your server administrator. For app privacy questions, contact the contact email listed on the OtoHari Google Play page. Do not email passwords, session tokens or private files.
